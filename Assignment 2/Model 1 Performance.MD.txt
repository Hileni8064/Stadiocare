## Interpretation

Mean Absolute Error (MAE) measures the average difference between predicted and actual Length of Stay values.

Root Mean Squared Error (RMSE) measures the average prediction error while giving greater penalty to larger errors.

R² Score measures how much of the variation in Length of Stay is explained by the model.
